EPISTEMIC BOUNDARY

Observability is not omniscience.

This system can identify a response that violates a consumer contract. It cannot inspect the ECB's unpublished upstream inputs, establish economic correctness, forecast exchange rates, or infer that a statistically unusual movement is erroneous.
